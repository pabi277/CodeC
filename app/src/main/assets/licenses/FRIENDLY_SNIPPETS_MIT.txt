CodeC — vendored snippet packs (Phase 30.1)
=============================================

assets/snippets/**.json are unmodified copies of files from
rafamadriz/friendly-snippets (MIT), pinned to upstream commit
6cd7280adead7f586db6fccbd15d2cac7e2188b9 (2026-01-23).

Re-vendor with:  python3 scripts/vendor_snippets.py

Only the packs for languages CodeC runs/colours are shipped; the
upstream licence follows in full.
