01 / THE WORKSPACE

Small footprint.
Room to do more.

A compiler is just the beginning.
Keep your tools, your projects and your next idea together.

01

A compiler. Already inside.

Built-in TCC makes C work offline on supported 64-bit devices. Tap RUN and Auto handles the engine—no picker, no setup just to start writing C.

Meet the compiler ↗
02

An editor that keeps up.

Recognizable file icons, TextMate highlighting, inline suggestions and 29 bundled snippet packs. Add Emmet, autosave and a projects hub: less friction between thinking and typing.

Explore the editor ↗
03

A terminal. And a window out.

A real VT/ANSI terminal with multiple sessions. Preview HTML projects in the app, then opt into LAN sharing to open your work on another device.

Find your first workflow ↗
04

Bring the tools you need.

Git, Python, Clang, nano, make and more from CodeC’s signed package repository. Install when you need them, with live status badges—not a forced first-launch download.

Inside the package hub ↗
YOUR KEY, YOUR RULES

A second opinion. Not the driver.

Bring your own key for optional AI help. Eight bounded tools can read or request a run, but never write or execute on their own. You review the diff and tap Apply; you approve each Run. Chat history lasts this session only.

Understand the AI boundaries ↗
06

Your work, with a way back.

Export all projects to a ZIP. Project-only backups, a crash-loop recovery guard and checksum-verified updates help protect your workflow. Git tells you what actually pushed—not just what saved locally.

OPEN SOURCE. OPEN ABOUT THE LIMITS.

Your workspace.
Your decisions.

AI is optional. For AI requests, nothing leaves unless you send it; when you do it goes to the provider you configured—there is no CodeC server. An approved task can send bounded follow-up reads. Every edit and run still needs your approval.
